Course Overview

The course introduces participants to the fundamentals of political leadership, democratic institutions, political careers and the opportunities available in public life. It strips away cynicism and provides a transparent look into how Indian democracy actually works.

Key Learning Areas

1. Understanding Indian Politics & Democracy: The multi-party system, ideological spectrum, and electoral realities.
2. Political Leadership Fundamentals: Qualities, ethical frameworks, and public accountability of a modern leader.
3. Constitution & Democratic Institutions: Separation of powers, Election Commission, judiciary, and legislative assemblies.
4. Political Career Opportunities: Pathways in policy, research, campaign consulting, media, and party organisation.
5. Public Speaking & Communication: Principles of effective political oratory, articulation, and debates.
6. Understanding Elections & Political Parties: How parties select candidates, manifestos, and voting behavior.
7. The Role of Youth in Indian Politics: Overcoming entry barriers and building credibility at grassroots levels.

Target Audience

Students, young professionals, aspiring political leaders, civil service aspirants, and anyone interested in entering public life.

Learning Outcome

Participants develop a foundational understanding of politics, public leadership, and concrete potential career opportunities.
