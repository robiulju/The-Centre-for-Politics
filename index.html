About The Institution

Democratising & Professionalising Political Education

The Centre for Politics (TCP) is dedicated to developing the next generation of political leaders, public representatives, policy professionals and changemakers.

Politics is a Serious Profession

We believe that politics is not merely about contesting elections or holding public office. It is a broad professional field that includes public leadership, governance, policymaking, political communication, election management, research, grassroots mobilisation and public affairs.

Bridging India's Knowledge Gap

India has millions of individuals who aspire to contribute to public life, but there are very few structured platforms that provide them with the knowledge, practical skills, strategic understanding and mentorship required to succeed. TCP was created to bridge this void.

"Politics needs trained leaders, informed citizens and professionally equipped institutions."

Professional Pathways

Career Opportunities in Politics

A career in politics extends far beyond standing for elections. Explore high-growth professional avenues across India's public leadership ecosystem.

Electoral Politics

Panchayat, Municipal, Legislative Assembly, and Parliamentary candidatures and elected office representation.

Political Party Organisation

State, district, and national organizational secretary roles, youth wing leadership, and cadre coordinators.

Election & Campaign Management

Professional campaign managers, war room strategists, booth coordinators, and voter turnout specialists.

Political Consulting

Independent political advisers and consultancy firms shaping strategy, alliance math, and opposition research.

Public Policy & Governance

Policy analysts, governance think-tank associates, government advisors, and scheme monitoring experts.

Political Research & Data

Survey researchers, quantitative pollsters, demographic analysts, and electoral data scientists.

PR, Media & Communication

Spokespersons, media managers, press secretaries, speechwriters, and brand positioning advisors.

Digital Political Strategy

Social media directors, digital advertisement managers, content strategists, and narrative engineers.

Legislative & Parliamentary Research

Legislative assistants to MPs/MLAs (LARS), bill drafters, committee researchers, and question-hour preparers.

International Relations & Geopolitics

Foreign policy think-tanks, international public affairs, bilateral delegations, and risk consulting.

Public Affairs & Advocacy

Corporate affairs heads, policy liaison officers, trade associations, and non-profit public advocates.
