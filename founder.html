Founder's Statement

Institutional Vision & Philosophy

The Centre for Politics has been conceptualised as a specialised platform for political education and leadership development, drawing upon practical exposure to India's political environment and democratic institutions.

The institution aims to combine political experience, academic understanding and professional training methodologies to foster a new generation of capable leaders, campaign specialists, and policy thinkers.

Core Objective

To establish a credible institution that makes political education more accessible, structured and professionally relevant across India — from rural Panchayats to the Parliament of India.

The Founder brings experience in political organisation, leadership development and public affairs, with an academic background in Political Science. Under this leadership, TCP is positioned not merely as a coaching entity, but as a long-term institutional pillar for national democratic stewardship.

Our Strict Non-Partisan Pledge

The Centre for Politics does not align with or endorse any political party, flag, election symbol, or partisan ideology. We are an educational institution dedicated to strengthening democratic governance, campaign competency, and institutional excellence across all levels of Indian public life.
