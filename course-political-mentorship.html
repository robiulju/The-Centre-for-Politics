Programme Overview

Every individual's political journey is different. This programme focuses on personal guidance, strategic direction, leadership positioning and professional development. Participants receive personalised guidance based on their background, ambitions, strengths and political objectives.

Programme Delivery & Meetings

Exact mentorship duration, meeting frequency and deliverables are finalised separately in consultation with the mentor to align with the candidate's personal electoral or organizational calendar.

Key Areas of Mentorship

Personal Political Career Planning: Crafting a realistic 5-to-10-year political pathway.
Leadership Development: Presence, executive charisma, emotional intelligence, and crisis resilience.
Political Networking & Relationship Building: Strategic alliance mapping across parties and civil society.
Understanding Political Organisations: Working the party hierarchy, lobbying for tickets, and cadre relations.
Public Image & Personal Branding: Authentic personal narrative, wardrobe, digital persona, and press relations.
Communication & Public Speaking: Masterclass in speech delivery, impromptu press bites, and debate composure.
Constituency & Community Engagement: Grassroots seva initiatives, grievance redressal cells, and volunteer mobilization.
Political Strategy & Decision-Making: Strategic dilemmas, political risk calculation, and timing electoral moves.
Long-Term Political Roadmap: Scaling from local governance (Panchayat/Corporation) to State Assembly and Parliament.

Target Audience

Aspiring politicians, elected representatives, political aspirants, entrepreneurs entering public life and individuals seeking senior political leadership roles.

Programme Outcome

A structured personal roadmap for developing an enduring political career and strengthening leadership capabilities.
