Programme Overview

The programme combines rigorous theoretical knowledge with practical ground exercises, strategic discussions, war-room setup and leadership development. Participants experience how election campaigns are actually planned and executed in India's competitive multiparty landscape.

Key Learning Areas

Political Leadership and Organisation Building: Building disciplined cadre, hierarchy, and delegation.
Election Campaign Planning: 180-day countdown roadmaps, resource allocation, and budget design.
Constituency Management: Profiling voters, caste/community dynamics, and localized manifestos.
Booth-Level Management: Panna pramukh systems, voter slip delivery, and polling day mobilization.
Voter Behaviour & Sentiment: Pre-poll surveys, issues prioritization, and anti-incumbency management.
Political Communication & Messaging: Slogan crafting, narrative framing, and opposition counters.
Social Media & Digital Campaigning: WhatsApp group networks, viral content, and sentiment monitoring.
Public Speaking & Media Management: TV debate preparation, handling tough press conferences, and rallies.
Ground-Level Political Mobilisation: Rallies, padyatras, corner meetings, and volunteer coordination.
Campaign Monitoring & War-Room Strategy: Real-time dashboard tracking and agile tactical pivoting.
