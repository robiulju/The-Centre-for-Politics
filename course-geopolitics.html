Programme Overview

The programme aims to develop analytical thinking and a broader understanding of international relations, diplomacy and strategic affairs. Participants will engage with leading diplomats, geopolitical thinkers, and defense analysts to decipher multilateral institutions, shifting alliances, and regional flashpoints.

Programme Logistics Note

Detailed duration, distinguished faculty roster, delivery format and programme inclusions will be announced separately to approved applicants.

Key Learning Areas

Fundamentals of Geopolitics: Geography, power distribution, buffer states, and maritime chokepoints.
International Relations & Global Power Structures: Multipolarity, unipolarity, and great-power competition.
India's Foreign Policy: Strategic autonomy, non-alignment to multi-alignment, and Vishwamitra doctrine.
India and Its Neighbourhood: Neighborhood First policy, border management, and maritime security.
China, United States and Global Competition: Trade wars, tech decoupling, and Taiwan straits tension.
Geopolitics of South Asia: Cross-border dynamics, terrorism, water disputes, and economic integration.
International Security & Strategic Affairs: Nuclear deterrence, cyber warfare, and regional defense pacts (QUAD, AUKUS).
Global Trade & Economic Diplomacy: Supply chain resilience, free trade agreements (FTAs), and sanctions.
Energy Security & Resource Politics: Critical minerals, semiconductors, renewable transitions, and Gulf energy ties.
Global Institutions & International Cooperation: Reforming the United Nations, G20, BRICS, and WTO.
Geopolitical Risk Analysis: Frameworks for multinational executives and state policymakers to anticipate black swan events.

Target Audience

Senior political professionals, policymakers, researchers, corporate entrepreneurs, public affairs professionals and individuals interested in strategic and international affairs.

Expected Outcome

Advanced understanding of geopolitical developments, international relations and strategic decision-making in high-stakes environments.
